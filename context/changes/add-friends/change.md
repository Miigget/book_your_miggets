## Notes

S-11 Add verified friends. Verified members send, accept, or decline friend requests to other verified members and see the friends list on the profile (including Add friend on someone else's public profile). Unverified accounts stay out of the graph. Out of scope: friends-only/invite-only run visibility (S-15). PRD: FR-019, US-04. Depends on S-10 user-profile (archived).
