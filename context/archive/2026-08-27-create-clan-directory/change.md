North star S-18 from context/foundation/roadmap.md. Verified member can create a clan (name, tag, profile picture); guests can browse all clans, open details (name, tag, picture, members, points), and see clans ranked by points (zeros until S-23). Prerequisites F-02 (clan-domain-schema) are already archived/done. Do not include friend invites, clan runs, officers appointment UI, or verified-finish points — those are later slices.
